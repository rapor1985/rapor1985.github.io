Channel Publisher

A private tool used by one creator to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

This application uploads and schedules videos, thumbnails, subtitles and playlist entries on the following channels, all owned and operated by the same person:

  • Slumberfall Stories (calm sleep stories)
  • Placer Sonoro (original music albums)
  • Lingo on Air (English lessons)

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
